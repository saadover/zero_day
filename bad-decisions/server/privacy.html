BadDecisions privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

BadDecisions is an Android app that picks between two options for you. This page explains what information the app uses and what happens to it.

What you give the app

When you ask for a decision, the app uses:

  • the two options and the question you type, and any extra notes you add;
  • the answers you choose to give about yourself: age, religion or beliefs, what matters most to you, your money situation and how much risk you like to take. Every one of these can be skipped or answered "Prefer not to say".

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and it has no accounts.

Where it goes

To make a decision, the app sends the information above to our server, which passes it to TypeSafe, the AI service that makes the pick. It is used only to produce your answer. Our server does not save it, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Your answers about yourself and your list of past decisions are saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or using "Delete my data" in the app's settings, removes them.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with other people unless you tap a Share button. Then the app hands a picture or message to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or Instagram.

Children

BadDecisions is not made for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Write to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
