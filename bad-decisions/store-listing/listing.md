# Google Play listing for BadDecisions

Copy these into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.

**App name** (30 characters max)

BadDecisions: AI Decides

**Short description** (80 characters max)

Can't choose? Write two options. It picks one and tells you exactly why.

**Full description**

Stuck between two options? Let BadDecisions decide.

Write your two choices, like "stay in my job or move abroad", "pizza or burger" or "text them or wait", and BadDecisions picks one for you. You don't just get an answer: you see how sure it is, the reasons why, and what the other option had going for it.

It knows you, not just the question
Answer a few quick questions once: your age, what matters most to you, your beliefs, your money situation and how much risk you like. Every pick is made for you, not for everyone. Skip anything you don't want to answer.

Share the verdict
• Share to story: turn any decision into a bold card for Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat or WhatsApp.
• Ask my friends: send both options to your friends and see if they agree.

Was it a good call?
BadDecisions remembers your past decisions and checks back later: was it a good call? Track your good calls and keep your daily decision streak going.

Private by design
No account. No sign-up. Your answers and your decision history stay on your phone.

From big life choices to what to wear today: stop overthinking. Make a bad decision.
