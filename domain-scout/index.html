How the score works: every name is rated on brandability, how easy it is to say, its extension and whether it uses real words. Your sliders set how much each counts. A name that includes a famous brand is always a Skip, because of trademark risk.

When Jev is connected, it rates brandability, the radio test, trademark risk and the likely buyer. Extension and real words are always worked out by the page itself. If Jev can't be reached, simple built-in rules fill in. Either way the score is a starting point, not a price estimate: always check availability, recent sales of similar names and trademarks before buying.
