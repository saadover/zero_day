How it works: match data comes from API-Football and is refreshed at most every 6 hours to stay inside your daily request limit. Jev is only asked when you press Predict, and each prediction is saved in this browser so you don't pay for the same one twice.

Once a predicted match is played, its result shows ✓ or ✗ under Results, and your hit rate appears at the top. Football is unpredictable: even bookmakers pick the right result only about half the time. Treat these calls as a second opinion, and only bet what you can afford to lose.
