.si Checker

Paste your list of words. Each one is looked up as a .si domain with the official Slovenian registry, Arnes, and marked available or taken.

Spaces are removed and ".si" is added for you. Slovenian letters like č, š and ž work too. Duplicates are checked once.

Paste your words on the left and press Check availability.

Where the answers come from: every name is looked up live with Arnes, the registry that runs all .si domains. "Available" means the registry has no record of the name; "Taken" means someone has registered it. You can confirm any result at register.si.

Couldn't check means the registry didn't give a clear answer, often because too many names were checked in a short time. Wait a while and press Retry. A name is never marked available without a clear answer from the registry.
